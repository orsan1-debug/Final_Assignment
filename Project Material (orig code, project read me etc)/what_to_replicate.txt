REQUIRED SMALL ADDITION - standard errors
- For one column of Table II (state which), report three standard
  errors for the class-size coefficient side by side:
    (i)   conventional OLS standard errors;
    (ii)  standard errors clustered at the school level;
    (iii) the Moulton-corrected standard error, obtained by inflating
          (i) by sqrt(1 + (m-1)*rho), where m is the average number of
          classes per school in your estimation sample and rho is the
          intraclass (within-school) correlation of the regression
          residuals.
  This is the correction covered in Module 5. Your figure for (iii)
  will not equal the paper's Moulton standard error, which also
  accounts for within-school correlation in the regressor; that is
  expected and is not an error.
